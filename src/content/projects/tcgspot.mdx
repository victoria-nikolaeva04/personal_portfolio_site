TCGSpot is a free platform for trading Pokémon cards with collectors near you: "No fees, no cut, no middleman." It started with a small startup team of four collectors, and I joined as the frontend engineer to shape how it looks, feels, and works.

## What it does

### Trade

- A map of nearby trade offers, so you can find collectors in your area
- Offers can go out to everyone or to one specific person
- Chat, meetups, and reviews to make trades feel safe and friendly
- The platform never handles money, because the trades happen between people

### Collection

- A binder for your cards and sealed products, in English and Japanese
- Lists, plus CSV import and export so your collection is never locked in
- "Be first to the deal" alerts for eBay listings near you
- Price averages based on current eBay asking prices

## How it's built

### Starting with the people

Before designing anything, I gathered requirements from 400+ potential end-users. Their answers showed what collectors actually needed and helped validate which features were worth building.

### From design to code

I designed the interface in Figma and then implemented it in Swift and SwiftUI, so the finished app matched the designs. Working in a cross-functional team meant design and development stayed in conversation the whole way, with teammates covering the CEO, AI and backend, and full stack sides of the product.

## My role

I was the Frontend Engineer, and the site lists me as the team's UX/UI Engineer. I owned the interface from design to implementation, and I kept it connected to the user research so that what we built reflected what collectors asked for.
