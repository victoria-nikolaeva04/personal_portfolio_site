AI Career Quiz, also called Career Helpi, is an interactive career suggestion platform. It showcases different job fields and advises someone on which field or job would suit them best, based on how they answer a set of questions.

## What it does

- Guides people through an interactive career quiz
- Generates AI-driven reports on industries, average salaries, and job positions based on their answers
- Showcases a range of job fields so people can explore, not just get a single answer

## How it's built

### The AI decides

The big design decision was that the final recommendation should come from the OpenAI API instead of a long list of if-else statements. The quiz collects a person's answers, and the AI turns them into a report that fits them.

### The app

Career Helpi is a React and TypeScript app, styled with Bootstrap and React-Bootstrap, with Framer Motion included for animation. It is deployed to GitHub Pages, so every update ships with a simple build and deploy step.

### How we worked

We built it following the Agile methodology, keeping the work organized as a team.
