AI is part of how I work now, both as a tool I build with and as a tool I build into things. Here is how it shows up for me.

## As a helper while I build

- **Debugging.** When I am stuck on an error, I use AI to talk through what might be going wrong.
- **Writing.** It helps me tighten up my grammar and content, like the About page on Arcana Noctis.
- **Learning.** When I meet something new, I ask for an explanation and then go try it myself.

## As something I build into apps

- **Arcana Noctis** uses the Gemini API to write a short reading from the three cards you draw, with guardrails to keep the tone gentle and safe.
- **AI Career Quiz** uses the OpenAI API to turn a person's answers into a career report, instead of a long list of if-else statements.

## What I keep for myself

I decide what to build, why it matters, and what the people using it need. I also read what AI gives me before I trust it, because it can be confidently wrong. The ideas and the final call stay mine.

I am still figuring this out, so I will keep adding notes as my habits change.
