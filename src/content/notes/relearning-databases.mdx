It's been a while since I last worked with databases, and I want to get comfortable with them again. The best way I know to learn something is to build with it, so I'm using a habit tracker as my practice project.

## Why a habit tracker?

It's small enough to finish, but it still needs real data decisions: what a habit is, how to record a check-in for each day, and how to ask questions like "how many days in a row?"

## What I want to brush up on

- Designing tables and the relationships between them
- Writing queries without second-guessing the syntax
- Deciding what to store, and what to calculate when I need it

## Follow along

The project is called Habit Calendar, and it's still early. You can see it in the [projects](/#work) section, and I'll write up what I learn here as I go.
