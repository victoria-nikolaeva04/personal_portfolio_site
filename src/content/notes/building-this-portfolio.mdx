I wanted a portfolio that felt like me: warm, a little whimsical, and full of plants. So this site is a small greenhouse, with leaves, sprouts, a snail, and a watering can you can tip over.

## What it's made of

- **React** with **TanStack Router** for the pages
- **Tailwind CSS** for styling
- **MDX** for the project case studies and these field notes

Every project and every note is just an `.mdx` file, so adding a new one means writing a file, not touching the layout.

## What I'm enjoying

The small details. A tiny animation can make a page feel alive, as long as it stays out of the way and respects people who prefer less motion.
